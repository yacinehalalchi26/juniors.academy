5. Practice tests and results

Formats. We offer three formats:

  • a quick test of 15 minutes in one module;
  • a module test of 60 minutes in one module;
  • a full exam of two or three modules taken back to back, each with its own 60-minute timer.

In a full exam you can't return to a module after finishing it. You can have one test in progress at a time.

Timing and inactivity. Timers can't be paused. When a module's time runs out, it closes automatically, and the next module starts or the test is submitted. If there is no activity on your test for 10 minutes, whether because you are idle or because you close the page, the test is cancelled and won't be scored. A cancelled test can't be restored, but you can start a new one at any time.

Free. We don't charge for any part of the Services. If we ever add paid features, we'll tell you the price before you pay, and separate terms will apply.

Practice only. Our tests follow the published format and syllabus of the official exam, and every question is written by our teachers and reviewed by CSCA examiners working with Juniors Academy. None are questions from real CSCA exams, and their involvement doesn't mean the official CSCA organisers endorse the Services. Your score is an estimate based on one attempt. It is not a certificate, we don't report it to any university, and it can't be used for admission, scholarship or visa purposes.

Results by email. We email your result to the address you gave when you submit, and show it on screen immediately. Delivery depends on your email provider, so we recommend saving the on-screen result if you need a copy. We may change the number of questions, time limits or scoring, for example to follow changes to the official exam.
