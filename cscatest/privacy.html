The short version

  • Juniors Academy collects your name, email, country and test results so you can take tests and receive your scores. There are no accounts and no passwords.
  • Your data is stored in Google Firebase. We never sell it, never use it for advertising, and send no marketing without your consent.
  • We record activity during a test only to apply the 10-minute inactivity rule.
  • No tracking cookies, only what's needed to sign in and to block abuse.
  • You can see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing privacy@cscatest.org.

This summary is for convenience. The full text below is what applies.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Juniors Academy (“we”, “us”) operates cscatest.org, currently available at juniors.academy/cscatest, and is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. That means we decide how and why your data is used, and we are responsible for protecting it.

This policy applies to the Site and to the emails and text messages we send from it. It is written to comply with Algerian Law No. 18-07 on the protection of personal data, as amended by Law No. 25-11, and with other data protection laws that apply to our users.

2. The data we collect

We collect only what we need to run the practice tests:

We do not collect your date of birth, gender, passport or ID details, photos, payment details, precise location, or any sensitive data such as health or religious information. Please don't send us such data.

If you don't provide the details the sign-up form asks for, we can't create your account or give you access to the tests.

3. How we collect it

  • From you, when you enter your details, take a test or contact us.
  • Automatically, when you use the Site. For example, your browser sends your IP address, and the test page reports activity so we can apply the inactivity rule.

We don't buy data about you or receive it from social networks, advertisers or data brokers.

5. Automated decisions

Two things happen automatically:

  • Tests are scored by comparing your answers with the correct answers.
  • A test with no activity for 10 minutes is cancelled.

Neither has any legal effect on you. Scores are for your own preparation and are never shared with universities. If you think a test was cancelled in error, contact us.

We never sell your personal data, and we don't share it with advertisers. We share it only with the following, and only as needed:

  • Google (Firebase). Firebase Authentication issues the anonymous session your browser uses during a test.   • Authorities, when the law requires it, or where necessary to protect the rights, safety or property of our users or Juniors Academy.
  • Business transfer. If Juniors Academy's business is sold or merged, your data may transfer to the new owner, and this policy will continue to protect it.

Our providers act on our instructions under written agreements.

We transfer personal data abroad only as permitted by Law No. 18-07, as amended. This includes obtaining authorisation from the National Authority for the Protection of Personal Data (ANPDP) where required, and using contractual safeguards with each provider.

8. How long we keep it

  • Your details and test history: kept so we can show you your result and improve the questions. Ask us to delete them at any time and we will do so within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep something longer.
  • Result email records: deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Rate-limit records: deleted automatically, at most one hour after they are created.
  • The anonymous session: stays in your browser so you can reopen a result you just took. Clearing your browser data removes it.
  • Backups: if we keep them, they are overwritten on a rolling basis within 30 days.

9. How we protect it

We use technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access:

  • encrypted connections (HTTPS);
  • a database that browsers can't read or write directly, so all access goes through our server code;
  • correct answers that never leave our servers before you submit;
  • limits on how many tests can be started from one connection;
  • access to production data limited to authorised staff.

No online service can be completely secure. If a breach is likely to affect you, we will notify the ANPDP as the law requires and tell you without undue delay.

10. Users under 18

Our Services are designed for students preparing for university admission. If you are under 18, you need permission from your parent or legal guardian to create an account, and you should read this policy with them.

If we learn that we have collected data from someone under 18 without that permission, we will delete the account and its data.

11. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used, which is the purpose of this policy;
  • access the data we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data, or have it updated;
  • delete your details and results;
  • object to processing on legitimate grounds, and refuse any marketing;
  • withdraw your consent at any time. This doesn't affect processing that has already taken place.

We offer deletion on request whatever the law where you live requires. If local law gives you further rights, such as data portability, we will honour them.

To use any of these rights, email privacy@cscatest.org from the address you used when taking a test. We may ask you to confirm your identity, and we will reply within 30 days.

If you're unhappy with our response, you can complain to the ANPDP in Algeria, or to the data protection authority in your country.

12. Cookies and browser storage

We don't use analytics, advertising or social media cookies, or tracking pixels, and our fonts are self-hosted. We use only what the Site needs to work:

  • Session storage. Firebase Authentication keeps your anonymous test session in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB), and we remember the details you typed so you don't have to retype them for another test. Clearing your browser data removes both.
  • Test position. During a test, your browser's temporary session storage remembers which question you were on. It is cleared when you close the tab.

Because all of these are strictly necessary, they don't require consent. If we ever add optional cookies, we will ask for your consent first.

13. Other websites

The Site links to other websites, such as the official CSCA site and juniors.academy. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so please read their policies.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Services or the law change. If a change is significant, we will tell you by email or on the Site before it takes effect. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent again.
