Questions

About the tests, your details and your results.

The tests

What is the CSCA?

The China Scholastic Competency Assessment is the academic test international students take to apply for bachelor’s programmes at Chinese universities. It covers Professional Chinese, Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry. Mathematics is required for everyone, and Physics and Chemistry depend on the programme.

Is this the official CSCA?

No. cscatest.org is an independent practice platform run by Juniors Academy. Official registration, fees and exam sessions are only at csca.cn, and practice scores can’t be used for admission.

What’s the difference between the three formats?

A quick test is 12 questions in 15 minutes from one module, covering every topic. A module test is 48 questions in 60 minutes, the real exam format. A full exam is two or three modules back to back, 60 minutes each, so 2 or 3 hours in total.

Who writes the tests?

Every test is a team effort. Competent teachers write the questions to the official syllabus, CSCA examiners review them for level, format and timing, and the Juniors Academy team builds and maintains the platform.

How close is it to the real exam?

Module tests and full exams follow the published format of the English-language science papers: 48 multiple-choice questions per module, 60 minutes, scored out of 100, with no calculator. The questions are original and written to the same syllabus.

How is my score calculated?

Every question counts equally. Your score for each module is the percentage you got right. For a full exam, your overall score is the average of your module scores. Wrong answers aren’t penalised, so answer everything.

Can I retake a test?

Yes, as often as you like. You get different questions each time, and the answer order is shuffled too.

During a test

Why was my test cancelled?

If there’s no activity on your test for 10 minutes, the test is cancelled and isn’t scored. Activity means answering, moving between questions, typing, clicking or scrolling on the test page. You’ll see a warning after 8 minutes. Closing the page counts as no activity. Start a new test whenever you’re ready.

Can I pause?

No. Like the real exam, timers run continuously once you start.

How do full exams work?

Modules run in order: Mathematics, then Physics, then Chemistry, depending on your choice. Each has its own 60-minute timer. When you finish a module, or its time runs out, the next one starts straight away and you can’t go back.

What if I lose my connection?

Answers save as you go. Reconnect within 10 minutes and reopen the test in the same browser to carry on. After 10 minutes without activity the test is cancelled.

What do I need?

A device with a stable connection, a quiet place, and paper and a pen. Calculators aren’t allowed in the real exam.

Your details and results

Do I need an account?

No. Enter your name, email and country, and the test starts straight away. There is no password and nothing to log back into. Your result appears on screen the moment you finish, and a copy is emailed to you.

I didn’t get my result by email.

Check your spam folder first. Your result is also on screen as soon as you finish, so take a screenshot if you need it. If it still hasn’t arrived, email support@cscatest.org with the address you used.

When do I get my results?

Immediately on screen, and by email within a minute or two.
